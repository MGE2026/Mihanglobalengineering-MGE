01 · Who We Are

A professionally managed engineering company专业管理的工程公司

Mihan Global Engineering (MGE) is based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, delivering total engineering solutions to our valued clients. From mechanical, electrical and instrumentation equipment to sand, brick, cement, rebar, steel structures and solar systems, we source, deliver, install and staff what projects need — on time and to specification.

With a strong commitment to quality, safety and innovation, MGE is building a reputation for reliability and excellence, supporting sustainable development for both local and international markets.

Leadership

Meet Our Leadership管理团队

The people behind MGE's commitment to quality, integrity and on-time delivery.

Engr. Mohammed Ziaur Rahman

MANAGING DIRECTOR
总经理

Engr. Mohammed Ziaur Rahman leads Mihan Global Engineering with a clear vision: to be the most reliable local engineering partner for international EPC contractors in Bangladesh.

An Industrial & Production Engineer (BSc, CTGU; MSc, BUET), he studied in China and speaks fluent Chinese (中文) and English. This lets him work directly with Chinese contractors, engineers and management — without any language barrier.

With 11+ years of hands-on experience on Bangladesh's mega infrastructure and power projects, he has worked closely with leading Chinese EPC companies including PowerChina. His expertise covers highway and expressway electro-mechanical systems, power and energy projects, procurement, site coordination and project management.

At MGE, he personally guides partnerships, project delivery and client relations — bringing international standards, practical site experience and a strong network to every project.

Minakkhi Rahman

DIRECTOR

Minakkhi Rahman is a Director of Mihan Global Engineering and the Managing Director of Minakkhi Corporation, a Dhaka-based supply and import company supporting Bangladesh's mega projects.

She brings strong experience in supply, import and business coordination, helping MGE build lasting relationships with clients and partners, and ensuring every order is handled with care, transparency and professionalism.
